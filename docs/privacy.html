OMTake — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Data Collection

OMTake does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app does not use analytics, tracking, advertising, or user accounts.

Camera and Microphone

OMTake requires access to your device's camera and microphone solely to capture and stream live video and audio over the OMT (Open Media Transport) protocol to receivers on your local network (such as vMix). No audio or video data is transmitted to any external server or third party.

Local Network

OMTake uses your local network to discover and communicate with OMT-compatible receivers via Bonjour/DNS-SD. No data leaves your local network.

In-App Purchases

OMTake offers an optional one-time in-app purchase to remove the watermark. Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Apple and are subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Third-Party Services

OMTake does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
