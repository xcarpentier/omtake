Features

OMT Streaming

Stream video and audio over WiFi using the OMT protocol — the open-source alternative to NDI by StudioCoast/vMix. Bonjour discovery, zero configuration.

Quality Hot-Switch

Switch between Low, Medium and High OMT quality while live. No stream interruption, no reconfiguration on the receiver side.

Multi-Lens Zoom

0.5×, 1×, 2× presets with smooth pinch zoom. Automatic lens switching via virtual multi-camera devices. Ultra-wide to telephoto.

Thermal Management

Automatic quality downgrade under sustained load. Auto-stop protection at critical temperature. Built for long sessions.

Orientation-Aware

Hold portrait or landscape — OMTake signals the correct orientation to vMix in real time. The preview stays steady so your framing never jumps.

Persistent Settings

Resolution, frame rate, quality, source name, mic state — everything saved and restored on the next launch. No more reconfiguring.

Program

Red border around the image. Rear torch lights up at low intensity so talent knows they're on air. Visible from across the studio.

Preview

Green border. The camera is cued but not live. Switch between sources in vMix and OMTake follows in real time.

Built on OMT

Open Media Transport is the open-source video transport protocol by StudioCoast, the makers of vMix. Lower overhead than NDI, native vMix integration, and no licensing fees.
